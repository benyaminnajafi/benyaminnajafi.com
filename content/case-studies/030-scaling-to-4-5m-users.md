Spearheaded a complete platform redesign by establishing a robust, scalable design system to ensure long-term consistency.

- **Leadership:** Led a team of 7 to iterate and enhance the core product experience.
- **Continuous Discovery:** Targeted user needs by blending qualitative insights with quantitative data (**Heap, Clarity, PostHog, Metabase**).
- **Massive Impact:** Fueled hyper-growth by scaling the platform's user base from **700,000 to over 4.5 million**.
