I led as Product Design Lead with a dual mandate: build the team and modernize the product without disrupting a live operation. I assembled the product design team end-to-end defining roles, hiring and onboarding, and setting rituals (crits, design reviews, async specs) that tightened our collaboration with Product & Engineering.

In parallel, I architected a new design system (tokens, components, accessibility, content guidelines) with clear governance and versioning, enabling consistent, scalable UI across surfaces.

To de-risk change, I audited and documented every current-state flow, clarified ownership, and established canonical user journeys. I then planned and managed a phased migration to the new system rollouts, compatibility tracking, and team training so we could evolve the experience step by step while maintaining service continuity.
