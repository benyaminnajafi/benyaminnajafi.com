To ensure seamless delivery from design to deployment, I managed the entire product development process end-to-end.

My responsibilities included handing over finalized designs to infrastructure, backend, frontend, and QA teams, as well as supervising the planning, testing, and release phases.

By overseeing the full workflow, I ensured execution was efficient, high-quality, and fully aligned with both user needs and business objectives.
