Spearheaded the zero-to-one product and service design for a novel melted gold trading and storage platform, tasked with navigating established market limitations and shaping a strong Go-To-Market (GTM) strategy:

- **Accelerated Product Delivery:** Partnered cross-functionally with two 10x engineers, rapid ideation, swift decision-making & seamless implementation;
- **Strategic Market Positioning:** Conducted comprehensive UX audits of all competitors to analyze qualitative and quantitative market realities;
- **Scaled Team Infrastructure:** Recruited and onboarded the foundational product and engineering team, including Senior Product Designers and Frontend Developers.
